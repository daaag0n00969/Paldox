## 1. Overview

Paldox is an **offline-first** Android companion for the video game Palworld. It is designed so that **core features work without an internet connection** after installation.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how it is stored, and what we do **not** collect.

By installing or using Paldox, you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

---

## 2. Who we are

Paldox is an independent **fan-made** project. It is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the publishers or rights holders of Palworld.

---

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1 Data stored only on your device

The app may store the following **locally** on your device (Room database, DataStore, and/or SharedPreferences):

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Language preference (e.g. English / Russian) | UI localization |
| Theme preference (light / dark) | Appearance |
| “Owned” flags for pals you mark | Personal collection helper |
| Personal notes on guides (if you write them) | Your optional notes |
| Local game reference data (pals, skills, items, etc.) | Offline encyclopedia / calculators |

This data **does not leave your device** through the app, because Paldox does **not** include a backend server, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or account system in the current version.

### 3.2 Data we do **not** collect

In the current version, Paldox does **not**:

- Create user accounts or require login  
- Collect your name, email, phone number, or payment details  
- Track your location  
- Access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera  
- Send personal data to our servers (we do not operate an app backend)  
- Show third-party ads or use ad identifiers  
- Use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or similar remote telemetry by default  

### 3.3 Network access

The Android package is built **without** the `INTERNET` permission for core operation. Opening legal document links from the About screen uses your device’s browser (or other app), which has its own network access and privacy practices.

## 4. Backups

If Android Auto Backup / device transfer is enabled by the system, **preference settings** may be included in a system backup according to the app’s backup rules. Local databases with regenerable reference data are excluded where rules allow. Backups are controlled by **Google / device OEM policies**, not by a Paldox cloud service.

## 5. Children

Paldox is a general entertainment/reference tool. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a future online feature, contact us via GitHub Issues.

## 6. Legal bases (EEA/UK, where applicable)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, processing of local preferences is based on:

- **Legitimate interests** / **contractual necessity** to provide the features you request, and/or  
- **Consent**, where required for optional features.

Because data stays on device and is not transmitted by us, many controller/processor transfer rules do not apply in practice for the current offline build.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or reset the device. Uninstalling Paldox removes app-private storage on your device (subject to system backups you may have created).
